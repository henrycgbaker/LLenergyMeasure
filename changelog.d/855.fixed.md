`TransformersEngine.load_model` now wraps model/tokenizer construction failures in `EngineError` (naming the model and engine), at parity with the vllm and tensorrt plugins, so a missing/gated model, OOM, or bad dtype surfaces as one actionable error rather than a bare traceback. Separately, when energy auto-selection finds no available sampler, the per-sampler probe reasons are now folded into the structured `energy_measurement_unavailable` warning, not only logged.
