**Breaking (results bundle):** the three independent per-artefact `schema_version` counters are replaced by a single `bundle_version` stamped into `result.json`, `config.json`, and the system sidecar, so one number covers the on-disk layout, the artefact set, and each artefact's schema as one contract. `ExperimentResult.schema_version` is renamed `bundle_version`. Earlier bundles read best-effort (the retired `schema_version` key is dropped on load); no converter tooling is provided.
