A key under `<engine>.engine_params` that is on no part of the engine surface (neither the introspected inventory for the pinned version nor the curated fields) is now rejected at config validation, naming the key and the closest matching field, instead of parsing and forwarding. Previously a misspelt key was silent: a sweep over `vllm.engine_params.max_num_seq` (for `max_num_seqs`) expanded into three distinctly-hashed experiments that all measured the same engine configuration, and the whole sweep ran. Nested blocks are checked as deep as the schema snapshot describes them; a block it leaves opaque still admits any key. Engines whose surface is open-ended (transformers, whose loader takes `**kwargs`) and installs shipping no snapshot for the engine warn instead of rejecting.
