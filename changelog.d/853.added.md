A new `BundleWriter` (`llenergymeasure.results.bundle`) owns the per-experiment results-bundle write policy in one place, driven by a declarative artefact registry (`ARTEFACTS` in `llenergymeasure.domain.bundle_artefacts`). A completed experiment that references a timeseries whose Parquet did not land in the bundle now warns at write time rather than only at read time; adding a future bundle artefact is one registry entry plus one writer method.
