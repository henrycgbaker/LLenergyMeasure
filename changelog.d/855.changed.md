The TensorRT HF pre-quantised-checkpoint gate (AWQ/GPTQ rejection unless a prebuilt `engine_path` is supplied) is folded into the tensorrt plugin's `check_hardware` hook, so preflight routes every engine uniformly through one compatibility seam (behaviour unchanged: same error, same message). Separately, the vLLM `gpu_memory_utilization` pre-allocation heuristic is extracted from `run_inference` to a named, unit-tested `_peak_matches_vllm_prealloc` helper.
